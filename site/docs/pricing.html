Common questions

Is billing per user?

No — Pro is a flat price per Teams tenant. No seat counting; add the whole team.

What happens at the Free subscription limit?

You can keep using your existing subscriptions; creating a 4th prompts an upgrade. Nothing you've already set up stops working.

Do I lose data if I downgrade?

No. Your subscriptions and history stay put; Pro-only features simply become read-only until you re-upgrade.

See the FAQ for data handling and security, or the features guide for what each capability does.
