What it does, in one line

Your pipeline POSTs a webhook to a private ingest URL; we normalize it into a rich Adaptive Card and deliver it to a Teams channel, a person's DM, or an incoming-webhook URL — with routing, noise controls, and an audit trail. The drop-in successor to deprecated Office 365 Connectors.
