Integrations

Sixteen CI/CD and observability platforms, plus any JSON webhook — one Teams app. Pick your source for setup steps.

GitHub Actions

Turn GitHub Actions workflow runs into rich Teams cards — no more scraping the Actions tab.

GitLab CI/CD

Pipeline and deployment events from GitLab, delivered to the right Teams channel.

Jenkins

Jenkins job results in Teams via the standard Notification Plugin — no scripting required.

Azure DevOps

Azure Pipelines run results in Teams via Service Hooks — YAML or classic builds.

Bitbucket Pipelines

Bitbucket Pipelines build statuses, delivered as clean Teams cards.

CircleCI

CircleCI workflow results in Teams, with HMAC-signed webhook verification.

Buildkite

Buildkite build events, normalized into Teams cards alongside every other platform.

Argo CD

Argo CD sync and health events, delivered to Teams as deployment cards.

TeamCity

TeamCity build results in Teams — via a native webhook or the tcWebHooks plugin.

Datadog

Datadog monitor alerts in Teams — routed, deduped, and escalated like any other event.

Grafana

Grafana alerts in Teams — firing and resolved, routed and deduped like any other event.

Drone CI

Drone CI build results in Teams — a fixed payload, zero template config.

Sentry

Sentry issues and alerts in Teams — errors surfaced where your team already is.

PagerDuty

PagerDuty incidents in Teams — triggered, acknowledged, and resolved, in the channel.

Prometheus Alertmanager

Prometheus Alertmanager alerts in Teams — firing and resolved, no template config.

Octopus Deploy

Octopus Deploy deployment results in Teams — a favorite of Microsoft-stack teams.

Any JSON Webhook

Datadog, custom scripts, home-grown CI — anything that POSTs JSON becomes a Teams card.
