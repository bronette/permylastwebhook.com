Integrations / Sentry

Sentry notifications in Microsoft Teams

Sentry issues and alerts in Teams — errors surfaced where your team already is. Per My Last Webhook normalizes Sentry events into rich Adaptive Cards and posts them to a Teams channel, a person's DM, or a webhook — the successor to Office 365 Connectors.

Install for Teams Read the guide

How it works

  1. Sentry POSTs a webhook to your private ingest URL when a build runs.
  2. We normalize it — the same card format as every other platform.
  3. The card lands in your chosen Teams destination, with routing and noise controls applied.

Set up in minutes

Create a subscription in the app (platform: Sentry), copy its ingest URL, then:

1

Create a subscription (platform Sentry) and copy its ingest URL.

2

In Sentry, add an Alert rule with a webhook / internal-integration action.

3

Point it at the ingest URL with a JSON body (title, level, project — see the reference).

4

Trigger the alert → the card lands.

See it instantly

Don't want to wait for a build? Hit Test on the subscription — a card lands immediately. Full steps in the platform reference.
