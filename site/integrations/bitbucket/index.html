Integrations / Bitbucket

Bitbucket Pipelines notifications in Microsoft Teams

Bitbucket Pipelines build statuses, delivered as clean Teams cards. Per My Last Webhook normalizes Bitbucket events into rich Adaptive Cards and posts them to a Teams channel, a person's DM, or a webhook — the successor to Office 365 Connectors.

Install for Teams Read the guide

How it works

  1. Bitbucket POSTs a webhook to your private ingest URL when a build runs.
  2. We normalize it — the same card format as every other platform.
  3. The card lands in your chosen Teams destination, with routing and noise controls applied.

Set up in minutes

Create a subscription in the app (platform: Bitbucket), copy its ingest URL, then:

1

Repo → Repository settings → Webhooks → Add webhook.

2

URL: your ingest URL.

3

Triggers → Build: Commit status created and updated.

4

Save.

See it instantly

Don't want to wait for a build? Hit Test on the subscription — a card lands immediately. Full steps in the platform reference.
